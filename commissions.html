Commissions & Contract Work

Hi, I'm Minh (@Senthemodder). Alongside my regular studio work, I am happy to take on both standalone commissions and longer contract projects involving Minecraft Bedrock add-ons, Java mods (Fabric / NeoForge), Java-to-Bedrock ports, 3D builds, or heavier custom systems like liquids, moving contraptions, and worldgen.

For long-term studio contracts, my rate is $18 – $45 / hr (or per-milestone), and rev-share or business opportunities are welcome too if the project has a solid plan behind it. Everything below is a flexible ballpark range and open to negotiation:

How I Work

  • Prices are negotiable: If you're an indie creator or working with a tighter budget, tell me what you're aiming to spend and we can adjust the scope or work something out.
  • Rev-share & business opportunities: Open to revenue-share splits (or hybrid base + rev-share), joint Marketplace releases, and other business partnerships with studios, creators, or serious teams.
  • Long studio contracts: Happy to join your team for multi-week or multi-month projects ($18 – $45/hr or milestone splits), sign an NDA, and work directly in your Git repo.
  • Deposits: Usually 50% upfront and 50% on delivery for smaller commissions, or split across milestones on bigger projects.
  • Payment: Wise, Bank Transfer, or PayPal (USD or EUR).
  • Bug fixes: Anything broken or lagging in what we agreed on gets fixed for free.
