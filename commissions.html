How Commissions Work & Terms

Straightforward milestones, regular video check-ins, and clean source handoff.

4-Step Delivery Workflow

  • 1. Scope & Written Quote: Message me your idea or design doc on Discord or email. I reply within 24 hours with a clear item list, fixed price (or hourly cap), and delivery date.
  • 2. 50% Kickoff Deposit: A 50% deposit locks your build slot. Larger studio contracts ($1,500+) can split into 40% / 30% / 30% milestones.
  • 3. In-Game Progress Clips: You get playable test builds and recorded clips as each mechanic comes online so feedback happens early, not at the deadline.
  • 4. Final Handoff & QA Support: Full pack/mod files and clean source are delivered upon final payment, backed by 14 days of free bug-fix support (plus Marketplace QA fixes on commercial contracts).

Payment, Rights & Revisions

  • Payment Methods: Bank Transfer / Wire, Wise, or PayPal (invoiced in USD or EUR).
  • Commercial & Marketplace Rights: Full commercial publishing rights are included on Studio, Add-On, Mod, Worldgen, and Engine commissions once paid in full.
  • Bug Fixes vs. New Scope: Any bug or frame-rate issue inside the agreed spec is fixed free of charge. Extra mobs, blocks, or new mechanics requested mid-project are quoted cleanly as an add-on milestone.
  • Confidentiality / NDA: Happy to sign studio NDAs before reviewing internal design documents, repositories, or unreleased Marketplace pitches.
