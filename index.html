About Me

Hi, I am Minh Lê (Designation Vee · @Senthemodder), a developer from Vietnam who specializes in software development, systems engineering, compiler & porting toolchains, simulation engines, and cross-platform game architecture.

I architect production-grade systems, automated build pipelines, and high-performance gameplay infrastructure across commercial studios (Pixelusion, Compycraft, Decayed Team) and large-scale open-source ecosystems (Galacticraft).

  • • Simulation & Engine Architecture: Real-time physics, procedural generation, state machines & simulation logic
  • • DevOps, QA & Automation Tooling: CLI build tools, automated integration testing, custom linters & CI/CD pipelines
  • • Full-Stack Technical Delivery: End-to-end product development from system design to commercial deployment
